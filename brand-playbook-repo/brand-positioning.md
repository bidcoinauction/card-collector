# Brand Positioning: America Emotionally Discovering Football

## Core Narrative
This is **not** a generic soccer highlights account.

It is the account that narrates how America emotionally discovers football before and during the 2026 World Cup.

**Core identity:** Football explained through emotion, aura, pressure, culture, and moments.

## Phase 1 (Now → May 31, 2026)
### Goal
Train the algorithm before chasing virality.

### KPI focus
- Completion rate
- Rewatches
- Shares
- Saves
- Comments

## Platform Priority
1. TikTok
2. Instagram Reels
3. YouTube Shorts

## Brand Naming Direction
Avoid generic handles.

Use cinematic, memorable, emotionally branded naming, for example:
- FinalWhistleUSA
- FeverPitchUS
- ColdNinety
- TunnelVisionFC
- AddedTimeUSA
- LastMinuteScenes
- TouchlineCinema
- FootballFeelsDifferent
- NinetyMinuteAura
- AmericaFindsFootball

## Visual Identity
### Palette
- Black
- Cream
- Muted white
- Subtle gold
- Film grain
- Filmic contrast

### Avoid
- Bright sports graphics
- ESPN overlays
- Meme fonts

## Editing Formula
- 0–2s: emotional hook
- 2–8s: tension build
- 8–18s: payoff moment
- Final 2s: emotional aftermath

## 4 Content Pillars
1. **America Isn’t Ready For This** (ultras, chants, anthems, crowd shock)
2. **Football Aura** (mythic player edits)
3. **Football Emotion** (grief, pressure, pride, reactions)
4. **Future America** (MLS, Messi effect, US culture growth)

## Posting Cadence (May 6 → May 31)
- 2–3 clips/day
- Clip 1: emotion/atmosphere
- Clip 2: player aura
- Clip 3 (optional): chaos/meme/reaction

## Caption Rule
Frame **meaning**, not description.

Bad: “Messi highlights.”
Good: “There will never be another athlete like this again.”
